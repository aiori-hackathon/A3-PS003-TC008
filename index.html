FedTraffic System Architecture Flow

STEP 01

Junction Nodes

Local clients (Junctions 1–4) ingest hourly vehicle counts and maintain private temporal records.

STEP 02

Local GRU Training

Each node trains a 32-unit GRU model locally on a 12-hour sliding window for 30 epochs.

STEP 03

Weight Aggregation

Secure FedAvg aggregates model weights (108.5 KB per round) without sharing raw traffic logs.

STEP 04

Global Forecast

Updated global model deployed back to junctions to predict next-hour congestion with high fidelity.
